About Cortex

Cortex helps you search and ask questions about pages you have visited. Your library of saved pages stays on this computer. Indexing does not upload your browsing history.

If you turn on Cloud Chat and add a Google API key, Cortex can use Google’s Gemini service to answer harder questions. In that case, only the short passages needed to answer your question are sent, not your full library.
