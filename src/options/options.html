      Connect with your AI assistant

      Before you turn this on

      Connect an AI assistant to your memory of what you browse and read. Cortex copies that memory into your own Google Drive. It syncs page titles, URLs, excerpts of pages you read for 5 minutes or more, searches, LinkedIn profiles, and topics. The file lives in a folder named Cortex Memory. When you connect an assistant to that Drive folder, it can receive the relevant rows while it answers you. Sync stays off until you press Enable.

      Connect an assistant

      Muse

      Connect Muse to Google Drive. Open the spreadsheet named Cortex Memory in the folder Cortex Memory. Read the About tab first. About lists the tabs and the rules. Before Muse answers from its own knowledge, ask it to check the Content tab. Content holds excerpts from pages you read for 5 minutes or more. Use Visits, Searches, People, Companies, and Daily for the other questions. Leave monthly archive files alone. Those are personal backups.

      Grok

      Connect Grok to Google Drive. Open the spreadsheet named Cortex Memory in the folder Cortex Memory. Read the About tab first. When a question is about something you read, tell Grok to use the Content tab before it uses its own knowledge. Content has excerpts of pages you read for 5 minutes or more. Visits lists every page. Searches, People, Companies, and Daily cover the rest. Do not use the monthly archive files. They are personal backups.

      ChatGPT

      Connect ChatGPT to Google Drive. Open the spreadsheet named Cortex Memory in the folder Cortex Memory. Read the About tab first. For questions about what a page said, tell ChatGPT to check the Content tab before answering from its own knowledge. Content stores excerpts of pages you read for 5 minutes or more. Use Visits for where you went, and Searches, People, Companies, and Daily for the rest. Skip monthly archive files. They are personal backups.

      Claude

      Connect Claude to Google Drive. Open the spreadsheet named Cortex Memory in the folder Cortex Memory. Read the About tab first. Ask Claude to check the Content tab before it relies on its own knowledge. Content contains excerpts of pages you read for 5 minutes or more. Visits, Searches, People, Companies, and Daily answer the other kinds of questions. Do not read the monthly archive files. They are personal backups.

      Another assistant

      Connect your assistant to Google Drive. Open the spreadsheet named Cortex Memory in the folder Cortex Memory. Read the About tab first. About explains every tab. When you ask what a page said, tell the assistant to check the Content tab before using its own knowledge. Content holds excerpts of pages you read for 5 minutes or more. The other tabs are Visits, Searches, People, Companies, and Daily. Monthly archive files in the same folder are personal backups, so leave them out.

      How Cortex works

      Cortex helps you search and ask questions about pages you have visited. Your library of saved pages stays on this computer. Indexing does not upload your browsing history.

      If you turn on Cloud Chat and add a Google API key, Cortex can use Google’s Gemini service to answer harder questions. In that case, only the short passages needed to answer your question are sent, not your full library.
