Cortex

Welcome to Cortex

Search and ask questions about pages you have visited. Private, on this device.

Before Cortex indexes anything. Cortex builds a private library of readable pages you open in this browser. That includes page text, titles, and URLs stored only on this device. Sensitive sites (banking, health, email, login pages) and Incognito windows are skipped.

If you allow it below, Cortex will also scan up to 500 pages from your last 30 days of browser history to seed the library, and will index open tabs. You can pause indexing, block sites, or forget data anytime in Settings.

Optional Assistant Sync (Google Drive) and Cloud Chat (Gemini) stay off until you enable them separately in Settings.

How it works

  1. 1

    Browse as usual

    After you start indexing, Cortex remembers readable pages as you go. Pause indexing or block sites anytime in Settings.

  2. 2

    Open Search or Ask

    Double-tap Shift, or click the toolbar icon. On a normal site the panel opens over the page. On Chrome's own pages it opens in the side panel.

  3. 3

    Find what you read

    Search by meaning or chat with your library. Optional Cloud Chat uses Google Gemini only if you turn it on in Settings.

Keyboard shortcut

Double-tap Shift
ShiftthenShift Opens or closes the panel on the page you are reading
