Cortex

Welcome to Cortex

Search and ask questions about pages you have visited—private, on this device.

Getting ready. Cortex is saving your recent browsing and open tabs in the background. You will get a notification when the first scan finishes.

How it works

  1. 1

    Browse as usual

    Cortex remembers readable pages as you go. Pause indexing or block sites anytime in Settings.

  2. 2

    Open Search or Ask

    Use a shortcut or the toolbar icon. On regular websites the panel opens over the page; on Chrome settings pages it opens in the side panel.

  3. 3

    Find what you read

    Search by meaning or chat with your library. Optional Cloud Chat uses Google Gemini only if you turn it on in Settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

Primary
Ctrl+Shift+K Windows / Linux
⌘+Shift+K macOS
Alternate
Alt+Shift+C

To change shortcuts: open a new tab, paste chrome://extensions/shortcuts in the address bar, press Enter, find Cortex, then edit the command.
